# Monsters: Hebrew names and stats (beginner DM handbook, pp. 48–63)

These are the stat lines of the Hebrew "החוברת למנחה המתחילה" (based on Swords & Wizardry Core).
Use them:
- to give an English monster its Hebrew name: match by name and confirm by stats (HD, AC);
- to fill a stat the source leaves out ("as the monster in the rulebook");
- never to *replace* stats the adventure gives — the adventure wins; note differences in `special` if useful.

Columns: Hebrew name · English (S&W) · HD (`hd`) · AC ascending (`ac`) · attacks · save · move · XP (`xp`) · page.
"HD 1ק6" etc. means hit points rolled directly (write `"hd": "1ק6"`). "?" in English = not sure of the S&W name; match by stats.

Not in the beginner book (dragons have no stats there, p. 65). For any monster not in this list keep the adventure's own stats and name it in natural Hebrew; mention the English name once in `special`, e.g. `"(באנגלית: Rust Monster)"`.

Attack bonus: monsters attack with 1ק20 + HD (max +15); under 1 HD, +0 (DM handbook p. 45). The app computes this — give `atkb` only when the adventure states a different bonus.
